# Meta Pacific

## Register
Brand. The site is the studio: hospitality, villas, founders, lifestyle. Design is the product.

## Users and purpose
Developers, villa and hospitality owners, founders, and lifestyle brands who need a place understood and sold before anyone flies in. The job is to show the work, name the scoped services, and make it easy to start a project from Bali.

## Brand personality
Direct. Field-practical. Unsentimental.

## Visual commitments
- Palette only: cream `#ede9de`, black `#000000`, white `#ffffff`
- Type: DM Sans (UI, titles, nav) and IBM Plex Serif (body)
- Square buttons (`--radius-btn: 0`)
- Official lockup: `assets/logo.png` (light) and `assets/logo-on-dark.png` (dark)
- Quiet cream footer with Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp

## Voice
Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No em dashes. Do not invent stats, emails, offices, or taglines.

## Anti-references
Purple SaaS gradients, grainy poster marks as UI lockups, tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrows on every heading, AI caption lines under the logo, pill CTAs, identical card kits with soft grey shadows.
