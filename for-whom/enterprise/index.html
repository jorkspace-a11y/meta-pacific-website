Questions developers ask.

What does a developer launch include?

The open offer is virtual tour, FPV walkthrough, aerial footage, a sales-oriented property film, and short-form social assets. The landing page and tracking sit in the same brief when the project needs a place for the lead to land.

How does pricing work?

The Immersive Property Launch has a published reference price of IDR 25,000,000 for one launch system. Scope can change it. Ongoing work across overlapping sites is quoted after a review. We do not invent a monthly number before we have seen the sites.

Can you run more than one site at a time?

Yes. That is the usual shape of a developer brief. Capture is planned so overlapping launches still share a look. We take the work we can keep consistent. We do not stack sites until the look breaks.

Do you work outside Bali?

The studio is in Kerobokan. Selected work includes Nusa Penida, East Nusa Tenggara, and Koh Samui. Permits and access are confirmed before we fly.

What if the building is not finished?

That is a normal brief. We tour from the site as it stands, and from 3D renders when the rooms do not exist yet. A buyer should still recognise the place when they arrive.

What happens after the first cut?

You review the cuts and the tour before anything is treated as final. Changes that stay inside the agreed brief are part of delivery. A new site, a new story, or a second launch is a new scope.

Do we have to manage the production?

No. Send the brief. We answer on WhatsApp. Reviews happen on the cuts and tours, not on a call for every frame. You should not need to run three suppliers to get a film, a walkthrough, and a page.

Send the project. Tell us what is blocking the yes.

Place, timeline, unit count, and who you need to convince. We reply from Bali, usually within a day.
