Strategy

Brand audit, positioning, messaging, and a plan that names what the shoot and the page are for.

Who it is for

  • Developers who need a launch story before the build is finished.
  • Founders who have the work but not a clear way to talk about it.

Problems it solves

  • Production starts from a shot list instead of a sale.
  • The film, the tour, and the page tell three different stories.

Scope

  • Brand and audience audit
  • Positioning and messaging
  • Content architecture and funnel logic
  • A written plan for what we will and will not shoot

Not included

  • A campaign we then refuse to produce. Execution is a separate service if you want it.
  • Guaranteed inquiry volume. Strategy reduces wasted production, it does not invent demand.

Deliverables

  • Situation notes
  • Positioning and message lines
  • Production and page plan

Process

01

Review

We look at the site, the project, and what already exists.

02

Qualify

We name the yes that is blocked: a flight, a booking, a lead.

03

Plan

Shot list, page, and tracking written so production can start.

FAQs

Do you produce the plan as well?

Usually, yes, through the relevant production service. Some clients take the plan to their own team.

How long does a strategy pass take?

A single launch brief is faster than a full brand and funnel review. We say which one it is before we start.
