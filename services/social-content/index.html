Social media and content systems

Pillars, formats, and a production rhythm so the work keeps showing up after the launch film.

Who it is for

  • Brands that appear in bursts around a launch and then go quiet.
  • Founders who need a way to speak without inventing a second personality.

Problems it solves

  • No pillars, so every post starts from zero.
  • Launch assets that cannot be cut into a week of useful posts.

Scope

  • Content strategy and pillars
  • Formats, hooks, and calendars
  • Production systems and publishing rhythm
  • Founder-facing content from real conversation

Not included

  • A monthly dump of unrelated clips.
  • Growth hacking that ignores the look of the brand.

Deliverables

  • Pillar map
  • A first run of posts from production
  • A calendar the client can keep running

No. A shoot planned for social can stand on its own. A launch is cleaner if they share a look.
