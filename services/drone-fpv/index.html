Drone and FPV

Aerial establishing shots and FPV walkthroughs used to make terrain, access, and space readable.

Who it is for

  • Land and development projects that cannot be judged from a gate photo.
  • Resorts and villas that need the approach and the layout understood in one pass.

Problems it solves

  • Buyers cannot tell how the site sits, how you enter it, or what the rooms actually connect to.
  • A pretty aerial that does not help anyone decide.

Scope

  • Aerial establishing shots
  • FPV walkthroughs and spatial tours
  • Landscape and architecture from the air
  • Cuts that can live on a page, in a tour, or as short-form

Not included

  • Stunts for their own sake. FPV is a storytelling tool, not a gimmick.
  • A flight with no plan for where the footage will sell.

Deliverables

  • FPV walkthrough
  • Aerial plates
  • Short-form cuts from the same flight

FAQs

FPV or a conventional drone?

Different jobs. FPV is for moving through space. A conventional drone is for establishing the site. Most launches need both. We wrote that up in Field Notes.

Do you fly in Thailand and Nusa Penida as well as Bali?

The selected work includes those places. We confirm permits and access before we fly.
