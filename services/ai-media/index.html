AI video and generative media

Generation used to move iteration faster. Art direction, references, and quality control stay mandatory.

Who it is for

  • Projects that need extensions, motion, or alternatives without a second full shoot.
  • Teams who already have footage and need it to go further.

Problems it solves

  • Raw generation published as if it were the work.
  • A look that breaks the identity of the live shoot.

Scope

  • AI-augmented video and image generation
  • Scene extension, motion, interview illustration
  • Repeatable graphic and subtitle systems

Not included

  • Being AI-made as an achievement.
  • Unsupervised output. Taste is a filter on what ships.

Deliverables

  • Approved generated or augmented pieces
  • Notes on what was allowed and what was not
